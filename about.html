01 — ABOUT ME

Drawn to stories.
Driven by curiosity.

I’m Cecil Reign Verzosa, an artist and IT student learning to combine creative expression with a deeper understanding of people and the world around me.

MY JOURNEY

A change of place,
a world of ideas.

✳

I moved from another province to Mindoro to attend college and begin a new chapter.

Today I live in Bacungan, Naujan, Oriental Mindoro, where I’m studying Information Technology. My academic path began with Humanities and Social Sciences, a background that encouraged me to pay attention to people, culture, and the stories behind everyday experiences.

Art has remained an important way for me to think and communicate. I have joined poster-making competitions and other contests that celebrate creativity. Each experience gives me another chance to learn, experiment, and share an idea.

WHAT GUIDES MY WORK

Creativity with
something to say.

I use art to educate and advocate. My work draws on social issues, the experiences of other people, and moments from my own life. I like testing what happens when different styles meet—especially when traditional and digital approaches can build a richer, more personal piece.
